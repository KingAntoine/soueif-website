03 / OUR STORY

A family business.
A lasting commitment.

Growing by challenging our limits, since 1975.

Founded in Chekka, Lebanon, Soueif Trading is dedicated to technical filter bag production. Our work connects industrial performance with environmental responsibility.

We believe clean air is a responsibility. Every filter bag is a step toward safer work environments, healthier communities, and more sustainable industrial operations.

Explore our journey ↓

Five decades.
Always moving forward.

Our journey from Chekka to a wider supply network.

  1. 1975

    Soueif Trading is established in Chekka, Lebanon.

  2. 1980–1992

    Raw material imports begin from the USA and Canada.

  3. 1992

    First encounter with Andrew Webron Ltd.

  4. 2001

    Company registration with the Chamber of Commerce, Industry & Agriculture.

  5. 2005

    First seminar held at Florida Beach, Chekka.

  6. 2014

    Industry milestone: Andrew Industries sells its filtration division to Lydall.

  7. 2017

    Supply network expands to Africa and nearby countries.

  8. 2025

    Celebrating 50 years of Soueif Trading.
